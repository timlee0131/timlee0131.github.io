I am a second-year PhD student in Machine Learning at Southern Methodist University (SMU) advised by Dr. Corey Clark. I am interested in geometric deep learning, transformers, and their implications in domains like time series and language. In particular, I am fascinated by how the study of geometric deep learning can inform model design in LLMs as well as studying expressivity and capacity of different LLM architectures through both mechanistic and theoretical analyses.

See my [CV](/cv/) for my background and [Articles](/articles/) for my written articles outside of peer-reviewed publication.
